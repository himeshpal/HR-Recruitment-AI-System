# Northwind Labs

## About the company
Northwind Labs is a product company that builds logistics software for small and mid-sized retailers. We have about 120 people. Our head office is in Pune, India.

## Work model
We work in a hybrid way. Most teams are in the Pune office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, and work from home on Monday and Friday. Core hours are 10:30 to 16:30 so that teams overlap; outside core hours people choose their own schedule.

## Leave and holidays
Full-time employees get 24 days of paid annual leave, 10 days of paid sick leave and the public holidays declared for the year, usually 12 days. Parental leave is 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the other parent.

## Health and benefits
All full-time employees have group health insurance that covers the employee, a spouse or partner, and up to two children, with a cover of INR 500,000 per family. We also give a learning budget of INR 40,000 per person per year for courses, books and conferences, and a company laptop.

## Relocation
Candidates who move to Pune for the role receive a one-time relocation allowance of INR 60,000 and 14 days of temporary accommodation.

## Compensation
Salary ranges are not published in job postings. The recruiter shares the range for the role during the first call, and any offer states the exact figure in writing. Compensation is reviewed once a year.

## Hiring process
The process has four steps. First we review your application. Then a recruiter has a 20 minute call with you. Then there is a 60 minute technical interview, followed by a 45 minute conversation with the team you would join. We aim to give you a decision within 5 working days of your final interview, and the whole process usually takes about 3 weeks.

## Preparing for interviews
You do not need to prepare anything special. The technical interview is a conversation about your past work and a problem to talk through, not a memory test. You can use your own laptop and any editor you like, and you may ask questions at any point.

## Accessibility and accommodations
If you need any adjustment for the interviews, such as extra time, a different format or an accessible venue, tell the recruiter and we will arrange it. This does not affect how your application is judged.

## Probation and notice
New employees have a probation period of 3 months. The standard notice period after that is 60 days.

## Equal opportunity
Northwind Labs welcomes applicants of every background. Hiring decisions are based on skills, experience and potential for the role. Our screening tools never use your name, gender, age or where you studied.
